### `what`

Distorcate is a remote-first, multidisciplinary design lab exploring ambitious ideas across design, technology, medical diagnostics, aerospace, urban systems and whatever else the problem requires.

we are interested in things that are still too early, too strange, or too difficult to fit neatly into an existing category. these projects are often shelved because they are 'too ambitious' to pursue

### `how`

research → design → prototype → iterate

we care about rigour, but not gatekeeping.

you shouldn't need the right institution, title, or résumé to start working on something seriously.

good work is good work.

### `moonshot`

a moonshot is a problem worth attempting even when the path is unclear.

### `speculative`

speculative means working with possibilities before they become obvious realities.

### `design`

design is not just how something looks.

it's how something works, behaves, communicates, and fits into the world around it.

### `projects`

a medical diagnostic concept exploring faster, more accessible point-of-care testing for immune thrombocytopenia.

reduces cost from ₹ 15,00,000 to ₹ 500~ target
